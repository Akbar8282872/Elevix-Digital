// 03. PROCESS

How does an Elevix Digital website actually get built?

Discovery to launch typically runs 6 to 9 weeks for a mid-sized Next.js build, 4 to 6 for a focused WordPress or Shopify project. Each phase has a deliverable, a sign-off, and a clear hand-off — no scope creep, no surprise invoices, no "we'll fix it in v2" debt.

01

Discovery + sitemap

1 WEEK

Stakeholder interviews, audience mapping, competitor teardown. We translate the brief into a sitemap, content model, and a measurable success spec — what conversion rate, what page-speed budget, what ranking goals.

02

UX + design system

2-3 WEEKS

Wireframes for every template, then high-fidelity design in Figma using a real design system — tokens, typography scale, motion language, component library. Nothing ships to dev that has not been pressure-tested for clarity and conversion.

03

Build + CRO + performance budget

3-5 WEEKS

Engineered in Next.js 15 (or WordPress / Shopify when that's the right call), with a hard performance budget — LCP under 2.5s, INP under 200ms, CLS under 0.1. CRO patterns baked in: clear primary CTAs, single-column forms, social proof at decision points.

04

QA, Launch + Handoff

1 WEEK

Rigorous cross-device testing, SEO technical audit, and final Lighthouse verification. We handle the deployment and provide you with complete code ownership, hosting access, and a recorded training session.

Web development — your
questions, answered.

01 What does a website development company in Lahore actually deliver?
We deliver high-performance, custom-coded storefronts and business sites. No off-the-shelf templates. Everything is built to maximize conversions and load instantly across Pakistan.
02 Next.js, WordPress, or Shopify — which is right for us?
It depends on your traffic and scale. We'll audit your goals and recommend the exact tech stack that fits your market, whether it's headless Next.js for speed, Shopify for pure e-commerce, or WordPress for deep content marketing.
03 How long does a custom web development project take?
Typically 4 to 8 weeks, depending on the complexity of the integrations (e.g., custom animations, backend databases, or local payment gateways).
04 What is included in a Core Web Vitals / Lighthouse 90+ guarantee?
We guarantee your site will load blazingly fast on both 3G and 4G networks across Pakistan and globally. We optimize every image, script, and database call so Google ranks you higher and users don't bounce.
05 Do you provide ongoing maintenance?
Yes, we offer dedicated retainers to handle security updates, content changes, server scaling, and continuous conversion rate optimization.
06 Can you integrate local Pakistani payment gateways?
Absolutely. We specialize in seamless, secure integrations for Easypaisa, JazzCash, and Safepay, alongside Stripe and PayPal for your global customers.
07 Will our new website be mobile-first?
Yes. Since over 80% of local traffic is mobile, we design and code exclusively for a flawless mobile experience first, then scale it beautifully to desktop.
